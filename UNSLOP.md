# Unslop pass

Done with the unslop skill (by Damir Sokolovsky, @heydamir, CC BY 4.0), 25 November 2026.

## Audit

Slop level: light. Built against the signs from the start; the audit found one type problem and a few checks to confirm.

- Body text was set at weight 300, outside the 400 to 600 range, and read thin on the mint surfaces
- The hero openings card shows times that look real: confirmed it carries a visible "Sample openings" note
- Insurance list carries a "Sample list" note so no office ships it as fact
- No uppercase tracked labels, pill badges, glows, gradient text, emoji, italic emphasis, hype words, looping motion or 700+ weights found on any page

## Direction

- Product: the site of a family dental practice, so patients can see what's offered, what a first visit costs and request a time
- Character: reassuring, plain, punctual
- World: a bright treatment room, mint tile, an appointment book with penciled times
- Moments: the "next openings" card beside the headline; the coral emergency strip, the only warm color on the site
- Signature: rounded time-slot chips, used only for appointment times

## What changed

- Type: body at Lexend 400; headings 400, small headings 500
- Color: coral is kept to the emergency strip and the emergency call button; everything else is teal ink on cool white and mint
- Checked at 360px on every page: no horizontal scroll
